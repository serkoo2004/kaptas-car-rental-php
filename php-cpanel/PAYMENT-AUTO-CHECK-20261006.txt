AUTOMATIC CONFIRMATION
- The result page reads the authorized payment status immediately and every
  5 seconds. It never submits another charge or changes a payment status.
- A verified live bank Sale/Search finalizes PaymentIntent=PAID and
  Reservation=CONFIRMED together and queues one confirmation email.
  Admin confirmation is NOT required for a normal successful payment.
- A successful sandbox payment shows TEST_PAID, not a live booking.
- Invalid callbacks, uncertain bank results and stock conflicts are NOT
  silently marked successful. Sandbox callback diagnostic codes are shown
  on the authorized result page when available.
- Browser polling pauses in hidden tabs and stops after 5 minutes, offering
  a read-only retry. Bank reconciliation must also run independently below.

CPANEL CRON JOBS - REQUIRED FOR DELAYED BANK RESULTS / QUEUED EMAIL
Confirm the hosting's PHP 8.2+ CLI path first. The examples below assume
/usr/local/bin/php resolves to that version. Do not guess another path.
Use the full verified binary path in both commands.
Check whether these jobs already exist; do not create duplicates.

Every minute (* * * * *):
/usr/local/bin/php /home/kap1e2renonline/bin/reconcile-vakifbank.php >> /home/kap1e2renonline/storage/logs/vakifbank-cron.log 2>&1

Every minute (* * * * *):
/usr/local/bin/php /home/kap1e2renonline/bin/send-reservation-mails.php >> /home/kap1e2renonline/storage/logs/reservation-mail-cron.log 2>&1

The worker reconciles PROCESSING/REVIEW attempts using bank Search;
it NEVER resubmits Sale. Its minimum age is 2 minutes. CHALLENGE without
a valid callback cannot be converted into success by this worker.
SMTP must already be configured for the email worker.
Existing ReservationMailQueue/ReservationReceipt/Mailer services and tables
from the previous reservation email deployment must remain installed.
